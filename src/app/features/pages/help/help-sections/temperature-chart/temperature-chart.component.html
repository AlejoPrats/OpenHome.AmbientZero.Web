Temperature Chart

The Temperature Chart screen gives you a visual breakdown of temperature changes across different rooms over a 24-hour period. Use this view to monitor daily climate trends and compare temperatures between rooms.

Navigating Dates

Use the controls in the top-right corner to switch between days:

  • < Previous Date / Next Date>. Step backward or forward one day at a time.
  • Date Picker. Click the calendar icon or the date field (DD.MM.YYYY) to jump directly to a specific day. Click the × inside the field to clear the current selection.

Reading the Chart

  • Time (Horizontal Axis). Displays the hours of the selected day in 24-hour format (from 00:00 to 23:59).
  • Temperature (Vertical Axis). Shows the recorded temperature values across the range captured for that day.
  • Room Lines. Each colored line represents a different room or zone (e.g., Escritorio, Cocina, Habitacion de huespedes, Habitacion, Living).
  • Interactive Legend. Click any room name in the legend at the bottom of the chart to toggle its line on or off, making it easier to isolate and compare specific rooms. (Note: Include this bullet if your charting library supports legend toggling, otherwise you can remove it!)
  • Hover Details. Hover your cursor over any point on a line to view the exact timestamp and temperature reading. (Include if tooltips are enabled on hover.)
