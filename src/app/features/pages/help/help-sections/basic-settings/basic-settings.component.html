Basic Settings

The Basic Settings screen lets you configure core application-wide preferences, including your local timezone and preferred temperature scale.

Note

Access to this screen is password-protected by default. Currently, changes made here apply globally across the entire application.

Timezone

Select your Timezone. Choose the regional timezone used to display all timestamps, charts, and sensor reading logs throughout the application. Use the dropdown arrow to browse options or click the × to clear your current selection.

Display Unit

Temperature Scale. Choose the unit of measurement (such as Fahrenheit or Celsius) used to display temperature values across charts and sensor tables.

Tour

Take the tour. Click this button to launch an interactive walkthrough of the application's interface and key features—helpful for onboarding new users or getting a quick refresher.

Saving Changes

Save. After updating your timezone or display unit, click the Save button in the bottom-right corner to apply your changes across the application.
