to export the information from the database you must click on the "Generate" button, be aware that this is a resource consuming process, you may loss sensor reports and the application will not be operational until it finishes

if you decide to generate the backup, it can take some time depending in how much data it needs to export, you will have a progress bar displaying on the screen, if you reload the page the process will not stop, but the application will not work correctly until the process finishes
